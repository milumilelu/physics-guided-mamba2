Registered 2026-09-05 before corrected results. Retrospective discovery reanalysis, not independent confirmation. The original phase2_8 scripts/config/results remain historical. Corrected outputs are confined to outputs/phase2_8_r1 (quick: _quick).

1. Guard v2: every finite pixel must satisfy z >= min(base, 0) - 0.01 um. This preserves measured negative baseline noise without allowing the nonlinear model to introduce negative removal in positive baseline regions or worsen existing noise beyond the same 10 nm tolerance. No clipping. Training candidates are rejected if any training phase fails. Held-out failures are retained, assigned the invalid class for scoring, and block a physical model-improvement claim; raw distributions are also saved. No test labels or test validity enter parameter selection. This is a necessary admissibility guard, not a validated physical law.

Mean across held-out groups remains the evaluation statistic. Grid and G28-B1 delta/CI thresholds unchanged. Boundary/tied negative gamma is not evidence of a uniquely identified sign.
4. Stable array transfer uses explicit finite complex sum (v2); legacy array_transfer remains available for historical scripts.
5. Task24 adds native-coordinate sample OOF, train-null and scoring scale, scalar R2, and all subset fold assignments. Primary fits and scalar/ILR Q2 definitions retained. 6. Exact-repeat diagnostic requires all five inputs and distinct source identities. Four-input matches are explicitly labelled as such. A single repeat pair cannot establish lack of process control or an irreducible noise floor.
7. No new experiments are prerequisites for this correction; physics feasibility/effect maps/error atlas/statistical separability remain separately tracked research work, not passed gates.
