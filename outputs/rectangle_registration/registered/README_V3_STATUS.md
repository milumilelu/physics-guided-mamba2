generated by the v2 region/edge weighted registration before v3–v7 were frozen.

They are retained as an audit archive. They are **not v6 outputs** and must not
be used for Phase B. The best current automated candidate, v6, stopped at its
registration gate with 8 hard failures; it therefore did not overwrite or
export registered maps. v7 was rejected after producing 17 hard failures.
